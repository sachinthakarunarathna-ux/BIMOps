Signature features

Connected to the tools your team already uses

Progress that updates from Bluebeam, tasks you can update from Revit, and a team that stays in step. Three things that set BIMOps apart from a plain task list.

Signature

Bluebeam Sync

Link a task to a Bluebeam Revu PDF and let the markups report the progress. BIMOps reads the review status stored inside the PDF, so nobody has to update the task by hand.

Signature

Revit Sync

Link Revit sheets and views to tasks, then update a task's status, progress and hours without leaving Revit. It runs through a pyRevit extension that BIMOps installs for you.

What's inside

Everything a BIM coordinator checks every morning

One window for the work that usually lives across spreadsheets, chat threads and half a dozen folders.

Board

Drag cards between Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done and Blocked. Filter by assignee. Double-click to edit, right-click for more.

Timeline

A Gantt view of your tasks against the project dates, so you can see what overlaps before it becomes a problem.

Deliverables

Track what has to go out, who owns it and when it is due, next to the tasks that produce it.

Team and workload

See who is overloaded this week. Hours planned against hours available, with leave taken out of the capacity.

Analysis

Status breakdown, workload by person and recent activity on a dashboard that opens with the project.

Calendar and notes

A personal calendar and notepad kept separate from any project, plus a header clock for the time zones you work across.

Submission Manager

Get a drawing set ready to issue in minutes

Four tools for the clean-up work that comes before every submission. Pick one, point it at a folder, and preview before anything changes.

Reports

The Friday report, without the Friday scramble

Pick the week and export. Reports come out as PDF or Word, and timesheets as CSV for payroll or invoicing.

Your data

Local first, by design

  • One file per project. Copy it, zip it or put it on a shared drive like any other document.
  • Works offline. Nothing to sign in to, and nothing leaves your PC unless you switch on online team access.
  • Light and dark. Switch the theme any time. Press Ctrl+K to search or jump to a page.

What BIMOps is for

One workspace for the people and the models

BIM projects are run across drawings, models, spreadsheets and messages. BIMOps brings the day-to-day management of that work into a single desktop app, so the team can see what is open, who has it and what is due.

Task tracking

Tasks, a drag-and-drop board and a timeline for every project, with owners, dates and status.

Team sync

Workload against capacity, leave taken into account, and weekly reports the whole team can read.

Bluebeam link

A bridge between the drawings your team marks up and the tasks that come out of them.
